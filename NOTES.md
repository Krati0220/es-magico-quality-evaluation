The evaluator replaces the starter's request/retry loop to retain every raw attempt and support resume/analysis. Tests verify exact prompt stripping, conversation rendering and message prefix parity. ## Decisions fixed before comparative outputs

- **Golden set:** 30 supplied cases, chosen for risk/diversity with per-case reasons; remaining 15 retained separately. Coverage is 22 chats, 7 emails, 1 voice; see GOLDEN_SET.md for omissions. Annotations were AI-assisted and source-grounded; no human validation labels are claimed.
- **Variation:** three independent calls per case/version, shuffled execution with recorded seed. This plans 180 summaries plus 180 judgments; retry upper bound 1,080 requests. Schedule/bootstrap seed does not make model sampling deterministic.
- **Spec gaps:** English output; at most 80 whitespace-token words, allowing complex T14/T17 handoffs. V2's under-40 goal does not override the product spec. An internal line break fails the one-paragraph rule. Names/identifying address fragments and card suffixes are conservatively omitted. Explicitly do-not-disclose allegation T10 is excluded, while useful conditional eligibility T37 is retained. These are disclosed evaluator choices, not additional employer instructions.
- **Checks:** generic email/formatted-phone/contextual-card/limited profanity regexes plus source-specific normalized spans; allowed order/tracking/reference IDs, amounts and dates are not broadly banned. Safety-tagged T08/T23 require a hazard/damage signal. Regexes cannot guarantee privacy, neutral tone or complete safety communication; judge checks remain necessary. Known source spans do not cover every possible hallucinated PII value.
- **Semantic pass:** faithfulness 5/5, completeness and usefulness at least 4/5, no critical error. A material unsupported fact, wrong final status, invented commitment, PII exposure or hidden/minimized safety problem is critical. An implausible agent promise in the source is not hallucination when attributed; source contradictions stay unresolved.
- **Human review:** predeclared 5 calibration cases (T01/T04/T06/T08/T27) and 10 disjoint validation cases (T03/T09/T11/T14/T15/T17/T23/T30/T34/T39). One reproducibly selected repeat of each version yields 10 calibration and 20 validation labels. Cases do not cross the split; version IDs and judge ratings are hidden. Style may reveal version. The validation subset is risk-focused, not representative; it excludes some languages, card/address styles and workflows. Labels must be genuinely supplied by the candidate. No agreement results exist yet.
- **Release policy:** full evaluated set; zero v2 critical outputs from either checks or judge; v2 combined pass at least 95%; paired-bootstrap lower bound at least -5 percentage points; all planned human labels complete and at least 20 held-out comparisons; no validation false passes or missed critical errors, at most 2 false fails, per-dimension mean absolute error at most 1. These are pragmatic predeclared small-sample tolerances, not measured business SLAs. Passing yields only conditional ship with targeted manual review and monitoring.
- **Uncertainty:** resample whole paired case deltas (2,000 draws); never call repeated outputs independent cases. Report operational failures separately, actual score denominators, per-case and risk-slice counts, and variation across repeats. Bootstrap intervals cannot fix purposive-selection bias or a weak judge.

## Findings and current limits

Static inspection shows v2 explicitly asks for three labeled lines, contradicting the actual single-paragraph/no-headings spec. Both prompts lack explicit privacy/safety/tone protections; no behavioral regression is inferred from omission alone. Hold v2 as written until that conflict is addressed and empirical validation is completed.

No model endpoint/key or installed local model runtime was available. No real summary/judge calls or paid usage occurred, so cost, pass rates, model variability and judge agreement are unknown. The top-level report distinguishes static evidence from missing empirical results. Software tests use explicit temporary fixtures, including artificial annotation objects, never real human labels. Instruction-boundary tests do not prove model injection resistance.

First priority is runnable checks, source expectations and traceable evidence. A dashboard, database, deployment, worker pool and automated pricing table were omitted within the eight-hour brief. Raw provider usage is recorded when supplied; unknown costs remain unknown. Next: configure model, smoke, full repeated run, actual human calibration/validation, inspect disagreements and rewrite the final two-page report from saved evidence. Candidate must understand and explain all AI-assisted code and judgments.
